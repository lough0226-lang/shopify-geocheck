## 2. Google Rank Data Issues, Lighthouse Update & AI Opt-Out – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

**为什么值得写**：The article reports three concrete, actionable technical shifts affecting SEO practitioners: (1) rank data corruption from one-word SERP blocks and goto-link throttling—directly undermining trust in rank trackers; (2) Lighthouse 13.6.0’s lab score inflation due to Lantern simulation changes, risking false attribution of performance gains; (3) a coordinated but not-yet-implemented robots.txt AI opt-out standard that creates near-term compliance ambiguity for merchants using NoArchive/NoCache. All three have measurable impact on how Shopify merchants diagnose, report, and configure their stores.

**可用角度**
- Rank tracking tools are now reporting phantom drops at positions 9+ because Google injects unrelated one-word listings—not because your page lost relevance.

- Using NoArchive in robots.txt to block AI training currently works for Microsoft but will stop working after March 1, 2027—and Google doesn’t honor it for Gemini training at all.
- The shared __usage_ai-training robots.txt token is a coordination milestone, but none of the three companies (Google, Microsoft, OpenAI) actually parse it yet, making early adoption premature.

- Of those unrelated blocks, 96.5% began at position 9 or below.
- A related pattern is tracked by Nozzle using a different methodology and it reports its weekly share of impacted SERPs had fallen to 1.5% in mid-October, then rose to 7.6% during the week of Oct.
- Ahrefs&rsquo; referral tracker measures the source of referrals at the site level and shows Google accounts for 24.74% of tracked traffic across 118,671 sites during October with ChatGPT accounting for 0.36%.

**坑**
- Assuming the Lighthouse score change reflects real-world performance improvement without comparing field data (CrUX) alongside lab scores.
- Treating the shared robots.txt opt-out as functional today, when Google’s documentation explicitly states it’s not yet supported.

**本周可落地**：In robots.txt, replace any standalone NoArchive or NoCache directives with Google-Extended: no and Microsoft-Clarity: no (if used), and add a comment noting March 1, 2027 as the deadline to migrate to __usage_ai-training once support lands.
