**为什么值得写**：The article presents early empirical data comparing ChatGPT Ads and Google Search Ads — specifically revenue per session and implied ROI — with methodological transparency (sample size, controls, limitations, conflicts of interest). It’s not a product announcement or listicle; it’s a practitioner-relevant performance analysis grounded in real transactional data from 102 websites, directly impacting budget allocation decisions for Shopify merchants running paid acquisition.

**可用角度**
- ChatGPT Ads currently deliver ~30% of the revenue per visit that Google Search Ads do for U.S. Shopify stores — and that gap persists even after controlling for site, device, and week.
- The widely cited '$0.52 ROI per ad dollar' for ChatGPT Ads is not measured but reconstructed using volatile CPC inputs — meaning actual ROI varies drastically by bid strategy and audience targeting, not platform ceiling.
- 84 of 87 dual-channel Shopify advertisers in the study earned less revenue per session from ChatGPT Ads than from Google Search — suggesting early adopters are not yet optimizing for conversational intent, not that the channel is inherently inferior.
- OpenAI’s $1B run rate reflects scale, not share shift: Alphabet’s Search revenue grew 12% YoY despite ChatGPT Ads’ launch — proving these channels currently serve different funnel stages, not interchangeable audiences.

**坑**

- Assuming causation — e.g., 'ChatGPT Ads underperform' — when the authors explicitly state their analysis is descriptive, not causal, and advertisers self-selected into each channel.
- Overgeneralizing U.S.-only session-level data to global Shopify merchants without accounting for regional differences in AI adoption, query behavior, or checkout maturity.
