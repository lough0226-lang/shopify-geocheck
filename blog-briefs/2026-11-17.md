## 2. ChatGPT Ads Vs. Google Search Ads: What Early Data Shows via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

- 来源：https://www.searchenginejournal.com/chatgpt-ads-vs-google-search-ads-early-data/597125/
- 采集自：Search Engine Journal｜相关度 3

**为什么值得写**：The article presents early, real-world revenue-per-session data comparing ChatGPT Ads and Google Search Ads — a rare empirical benchmark for AI-native advertising performance. It exposes methodological nuance (e.g., the $0.52 vs $3.40 per-dollar claim is derived, not measured) and highlights advertiser behavior (100k+ advertisers on ChatGPT Ads, yet no observable budget shift in Alphabet’s reported growth). This is actionable for Shopify merchants evaluating where to allocate limited ad spend amid AI channel fragmentation.

**可用角度**
- ChatGPT Ads are not replacing Google Search Ads — they’re attracting new advertisers, not poaching existing search budgets.
- For Shopify merchants, ChatGPT Ads currently deliver lower transaction density (0.37% vs 1.17% per session) and lower revenue per visit ($0.89 vs $2.45), making them a supplementary, not substitutive, channel.
- The 'value per dollar' metric ($0.52 vs $3.40) collapses two variables — conversion efficiency and CPC — and misleads merchants who don’t control or compare their own actual CPCs across platforms.
- OpenAI’s $1B run rate reflects scale of adoption, not ROI parity: 84 of 87 dual-channel advertisers earned less revenue per session from ChatGPT Ads, suggesting targeting or intent alignment remains fundamentally weaker.

**可引用的事实**（原句照抄，写稿时直接引，不要改写数字）
- 31, OpenAI reported that its ads product had achieved a $1 billion annualized run rate.
- 2 coverage provided the pooled revenue metric from 102 different websites with transaction and revenue tracking.
- One of the authors&rsquo; universities also released a press statement saying that for every dollar an advertiser spends on ChatGPT Ads, they receive $0.52 back in revenue (compared to $3.40 for every dollar spent on Google Search).
- 21 engineering post from OpenAI puts the run rate above $1 billion, with more than 100,000 advertisers having run ads since launch.
- Alphabet reported $63.35 billion in Search & other revenue for the third quarter, up 12%, which I covered on Oct.
- Google Search was the most-mentioned potential source of future ChatGPT ad spending in a Bank of America survey of 117 advertisers and agencies, TheStreet reported on Oct.
- Out of 87 different advertisers who used both paid search and ChatGPT ads in U.S.
- About 20% of the 102 websites studied were services, software, education, or finance websites.

**坑**
- Treating the Frankfurt School's derived $0.52/$3.40 ratio as an absolute performance benchmark rather than a context-dependent estimate based on one account's CPC and median search CPC.
- Assuming the 102-website sample represents Shopify merchants — only ~20% were services/software/education/finance; none were confirmed e-commerce or Shopify-native.
- Overlooking that OpenAI explicitly stated the study reflects an 'earlier period' of ChatGPT Ads development, while Target CPA and other optimizations have since launched.

**本周可落地**：Audit your last 30 days of Google Search Ads: isolate branded vs non-branded campaigns, then manually calculate revenue per click (RPC) — use that as your internal benchmark before testing any ChatGPT Ads spend.
