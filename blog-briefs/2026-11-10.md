## 3. Google Rank Data Issues, Lighthouse Update & AI Opt-Out – SEO Pulse via @sejournal, @MattGSouthern

- 来源：https://www.searchenginejournal.com/seo-pulse-rank-data-problems-pagespeed-scores-ai-training-opt-out/596104/
- 采集自：Search Engine Journal｜相关度 4

**为什么值得写**：The article reports three concrete, actionable technical shifts affecting SEO practitioners: (1) rank data corruption from one-word SERP noise and goto-link throttling—directly undermining trust in rank trackers; (2) Lighthouse 13.6.0’s lab score volatility due to Lantern simulation changes, creating false attribution risk for performance claims; (3) a coordinated but non-operational robots.txt AI opt-out standard that misleads merchants into thinking compliance is immediate or universal. All three have measurable consequences for how Shopify merchants interpret data, configure robots.txt, and validate optimizations.

**可用角度**
- Rank tracking tools are now reporting phantom drops at positions 9+ because Google injects unrelated one-word listings—not algorithmic demotion, but data pollution that inflates churn metrics
- Lighthouse 13.6.0’s Lantern update means PageSpeed score increases on unchanged pages are likely simulation artifacts, not real-world improvements, especially for JavaScript-heavy Shopify themes
- The shared __usage_ai-training robots.txt token is functionally inert today: Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI all publicly state they do not yet honor it, making early adoption premature and potentially harmful if it overrides existing Google-Extended directives
- Google’s conflation of Gemini training and Gemini Apps grounding in its robots.txt docs creates ambiguity—merchants who opt out via the new token may unintentionally block app grounding while believing they’re only limiting model training

**可引用的事实**（原句照抄，写稿时直接引，不要改写数字）
- 3, 84 percent of the flagged cases were unrelated listings about a single word from the query.
- Of those unrelated blocks, 96.5% began at position 9 or below.
- A related pattern is tracked by Nozzle using a different methodology and it reports its weekly share of impacted SERPs had fallen to 1.5% in mid-October, then rose to 7.6% during the week of Oct.
- Ahrefs&rsquo; referral tracker measures the source of referrals at the site level and shows Google accounts for 24.74% of tracked traffic across 118,671 sites during October with ChatGPT accounting for 0.36%.

**坑**
- Assuming the shared robots.txt opt-out is live and functional across all three companies (none support it yet)
- Treating Lighthouse score changes post-13.6.0 as evidence of real-world performance improvement without comparing field data
- Blaming algorithmic penalties for rank drops at position 9+ without first checking for one-word SERP noise or unresolved goto redirects
- Applying the new robots.txt token without auditing whether it conflicts with existing Google-Extended or NoArchive rules

**本周可落地**：Audit your robots.txt this week: remove any draft __usage_ai-training blocks, verify Google-Extended is explicitly set if you want to allow Gemini Apps grounding, and confirm NoArchive/NoCache remain only where intentionally used for Microsoft compliance before March 2027
