**可用角度**
- Most Shopify merchants are unknowingly building AI visibility reports on tools with >55% expected failure rates within 2 years — not because the tools are bad, but because their underlying cost structure makes long-term operation unsustainable.
- A 'brand mention' in one AI visibility dashboard is not equivalent to the same mention in another — due to incompatible definitions of presence (yes/no vs. prominence/tone), prompt cadence (single query vs. multi-turn), and inference source (direct API vs. live browsing).
- When a Shopify merchant signs a 12-month contract with an AI visibility tool, they are not buying measurement — they are buying temporary access to a proprietary synthetic index with no interoperability or exportable ground truth.
